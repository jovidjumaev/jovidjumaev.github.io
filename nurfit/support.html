Common questions

How do I export or import my weigh-ins?

Open Profile, then Your data. Export CSV or Export Excel saves your weigh-ins as a file. Import CSV or Import Excel adds weigh-ins from a file; rows already in NurFit are skipped. Back up everything saves a full backup, and Restore a backup brings it back on another device.

The reminder does not arrive

Turn it on in Profile, then Reminder, and allow notifications when asked. If you declined before, open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap Notifications, then NurFit, and switch on Allow Notifications.

How do I cancel NurFit Plus?

In NurFit, open Profile, then NurFit Plus, then Manage or cancel subscription. You can also cancel in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. In NurFit, open Profile, then NurFit Plus, then Request a refund, or go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Plus on another device

Open the NurFit Plus screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile, then Your data, then Erase all data. This removes every weigh-in, note, photo and setting from this device. Weigh-ins already saved to Apple Health stay there; delete them in the Health app if you want. Deleting the app also removes all NurFit data. NurFit has no account and no server, so there is nothing stored anywhere else.
