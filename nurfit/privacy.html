NurFit Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

English · Русский · Тоҷикӣ

Summary

NurFit keeps all of your data on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is synced to iCloud. Like other app data, it is included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you use one.

If you add the NurFit widget, it shows your latest weight on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. When App lock is on, the widget hides your weight.

Data stored on your device

Weights, goals, notes, photos, body measurements and settings are stored on your device only. The developer cannot see them.

Apple Health

If you give permission, NurFit reads and writes body mass in Apple Health. That data stays on your device under Apple Health's controls. It is not used for advertising and is not shared.

Photos, camera and Face ID

Photos you take or choose stay in the app's storage on your device. The camera is used only for progress photos. Face ID is used only for the optional app lock.

AI insights

The optional AI insights run on Apple's on-device model. Your questions and their answers do not leave your phone.

Export and backup

Export and backup files are created only when you ask for one. They go where you send them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. Health data is managed in the Health app.

Not medical advice

NurFit is not medical advice. BMI and calorie estimates are general information. Talk to a doctor about your health.
