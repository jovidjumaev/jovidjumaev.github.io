NurFit Plus subscription

NurFit Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription, available monthly or yearly. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. The price is shown in the app before you purchase.

NurFit Plus is also available as a lifetime one-time purchase: a single payment, no renewal and no free trial. It unlocks Plus permanently for that Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at the end of the free trial.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple.

What Plus unlocks

Plus unlocks AI insights, Goal Planner and Recap. The rest of the app is free, including body measurements, widgets, photo extras and app lock.
